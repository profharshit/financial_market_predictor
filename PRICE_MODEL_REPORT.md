# Price Forecast Model - Selection & Validation Report

Generated by `train_price_model.py` (raw numbers in `outputs/price_model_metrics.json`).
Supersedes the 4,156-row results in `DOCUMENTATION.md` sections 5 and 8.

## Data
16,577 usable hourly EUR/USD bars, 2024-01-08 -> 2026-09-04 (about 4x the earlier dataset).
Walk-forward development on the first 85%; **untouched holdout = last 2,487 bars
(2026-04-14 -> 2026-09-04)**.

## What is predicted
For horizons of 1, 4 and 24 trading hours: the 10th / 50th / 90th percentile of the
**volatility-scaled forward return** `z = log(P[t+h]/P[t]) / (ATR_pct * sqrt(h))`, converted back
to prices. Scaling by current volatility makes one model valid in calm and violent markets and
makes the band breathe automatically. 25 stationary features, no price levels.

## Candidates and selection rule (fixed before looking at results)
`vol_only` (no-skill benchmark: zero drift, ATR-scaled bands) - `ridge` (linear) - `xgb_quantile`.
A complex model replaces the benchmark only if it has lower mean pinball loss and wins >= 4 of 6
walk-forward folds. It may drive BUY/SELL direction only if its median also beats the benchmark's
in >= 4 folds *and* its directional accuracy is significant (one-sided binomial p < 0.05, counts
divided by the horizon because overlapping targets are not independent).

## Results

Walk-forward mean pinball loss (lower is better), 6 folds:

| Horizon | vol_only | ridge | xgb_quantile | Winner | Folds won vs benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1h  | 0.1701 | 0.1706 | **0.1648** (-3.1%) | xgb_quantile | 6/6 |
| 4h  | 0.1781 | 0.1793 | **0.1731** (-2.8%) | xgb_quantile | 6/6 |
| 24h | **0.1884** | 0.1922 | 0.1903 | vol_only | none |

Untouched holdout (models fit on dev only):

| Horizon | Winner pinball | Benchmark pinball | 10-90 coverage (target 0.80) | MAE vs "no change" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1h  | 0.1630 | 0.1684 | 0.805 | 0.997x |
| 4h  | 0.1694 | 0.1745 | 0.815 | 0.999x |
| 24h | 0.1806 (vol_only) | - | 0.851 | 1.000x |

Directional skill (overlap-adjusted): 1h p = 0.051, 4h p = 0.197 -> **not established**; the
median forecast is statistically tied with "price stays put". Top XGBoost features by gain are
hour-of-day (`hour_cos` 0.163, `hour_sin` 0.154), then `range_atr`, `bb_width`, `atr_pct` -
consistent with the gain coming from **calibrating band width to the intraday volatility
pattern**, not from predicting direction.

Trailing-stop backtest, 1 pip cost per position change, always-in-market flipping on the stop:

| Strategy | Full sample net | Holdout net | Holdout Sharpe (s.e. ~1.6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buy & hold | +5.98% | -1.42% | -0.71 |
| Fixed stop (original Pine, key 3.0) | -11.52% | -7.17% | -3.68 |
| **Adaptive stop** | -11.80% | -6.71% | -3.43 |
| Adaptive stop + model gate (holdout) | - | -1.58% (gross +0.24%, in market 8%) | -2.74 |

**Verdict:** the trailing stop and the model gate do not produce a net-of-cost trading edge, and
the adaptive stop is neither clearly better nor worse than the fixed one. `edge_verified = false`
is therefore returned by the API on every prediction.

## Paste-ready paragraph for the report

> A probabilistic price forecaster was built for EUR/USD at 1, 4 and 24 trading-hour horizons on
> 16,577 hourly bars. Targets are volatility-scaled forward returns, predicted as 10/50/90th
> percentiles by quantile gradient boosting and converted to price bands. Model choice used six-fold
> expanding-window walk-forward validation with a horizon-length embargo and a rule fixed in advance,
> then confirmation on a 2,487-bar untouched holdout. Gradient boosting improved pinball loss over
> a volatility-only benchmark by about 3% at 1h and 4h (all six folds, and on the holdout) while
> keeping 80% interval coverage calibrated (0.805 / 0.815); no model beat the benchmark at 24h.
> Median forecasts were statistically indistinguishable from "no change" and directional accuracy
> was not significant once overlapping targets were accounted for. A volatility-adaptive ATR
> trailing stop and a model-gated variant were backtested with transaction costs and did not
> demonstrate a tradable edge. The system therefore delivers calibrated uncertainty bands and a
> risk-management stop level, and reports the absence of directional predictability as a finding,
> consistent with the efficient-market expectation for a highly liquid currency pair.

## Limitations
- The holdout is ~4.5 months (one market regime); Sharpe standard errors are large (~1.6).
- Adaptive-stop parameters (lookback 100, power 0.5, clip 0.75-1.5) were set a priori, not tuned.
- Costs assumed: 1 pip per position change. Real spreads vary by broker and hour.
- 24h band is over-covered on the holdout (0.85 vs 0.80): recent volatility was below the
  training-period norm. Watch `/forward-test` and retrain on fresh data periodically.
